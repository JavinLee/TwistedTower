## Direct answer

Twisted Tower follows a protagonist climbing an abandoned 1950s resort or theme park to save the love of their life. The climb passes through five themed areas while corrupted fairy-tale mascots, traps, puzzles, movement toys, and toy-themed weapons shape the journey. The collected ending research says that the rescue is revealed as a film production, Mr. Twister is Vincent's father, and Vincent was cast as the film's lead.

## Setting and goal

The resort is described as abandoned, 1950s-inspired, and transformed into a horror environment. Its five main areas are Hotel, Waterpark, Clown Casino, Carnival Forest, and Space Station. This structure gives the story a rising, surreal climb from a recognizable resort setting toward a space-station finale.

The official material frames the objective as reaching the top and saving the love of the player's life. Along the way, the player fights corrupted mascots, solves twisted puzzles, survives deadly traps, and uses bizarre toys to jump, dash, and grapple. The story is therefore delivered through movement, combat, environment, and route progress rather than only through dialogue.
