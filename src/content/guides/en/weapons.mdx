## Confirmed arsenal

The official Steam page and GamesPress release frame the weapons as absurd, toyish equipment for fast first-person combat. The collected notes mention the mallet, rubber-band pistol, Tommy Gun, shotgun, chain-gun, slingshot, and ray gun. These names are suitable as research-backed references, but the exact in-game spelling, alternate fire behavior, and damage values should be checked before a database is published.

The research also records area-specific equipment leads. The Mermaid Palace walkthrough records a shotgun route, while the Fairground Forest walkthrough records a Slingshot during progression and mentions a Cannon secondary fire, Marble Gun, or Slingshot as final-fight recommendations. These details belong to their source context and should not be generalized into a universal ranking.
