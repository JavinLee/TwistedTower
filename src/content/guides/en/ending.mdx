## Spoiler warning

This page discusses the researched ending of Twisted Tower and should be read after the final route. The supplied guide research covers Charlotte's Castle, Tower Top, the final fight, the camera reveal, and the film-production twist. It does not support every interpretation about the characters or confirm multiple story endings.

## Direct answer

The collected ending material says that the rescue scene contradicts an earlier condition: Charlotte has one hand removed earlier, but both hands are intact when Vincent reaches her. Cameras then appear around the scene, and someone calls “Cut.” The reveal is that the rescue was part of a film production, Mr. Twister is Vincent's father, and Vincent was cast as the film's lead.

This is the direct story answer supported by the collected sources. Questions about who knew about the production, how the filming was arranged, and how every route relates to the scene remain story implications or **&#123;to be confirmed&#125;** rather than absolute facts.

## Ending reveal

The camera and “Cut” moment changes the meaning of the rescue. The tower climb and the emotional goal are revealed as part of a production, while the family connection between Mr. Twister and Vincent reframes the final scene. The ending therefore links the horror attraction, performance, and protagonist identity without requiring a claim about multiple endings.
