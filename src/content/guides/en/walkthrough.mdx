## Before You Enter

Twisted Tower is a first-person action-adventure horror shooter set in an abandoned 1950s resort and theme-park world. The guide research used for this article describes a five-floor progression from The Hotel to The Space Station. This is a fan-made route companion, not an official guide, and the named floors, Journal areas, movement tools, and mode names below should be treated as guide research until they are checked in-game.

The research source is the third-party [Twisted Tower Walkthrough](https://twistedtowerguide.wiki/walkthrough). It is cited as research only and is not presented as an official source. The source gives the floor order, Journal area names, four movement tools, and several mode or secret references. It does not establish every room connection, item location, key name, combat encounter, puzzle answer, checkpoint, or unlock trigger, so those details are marked **verify in-game** rather than guessed.

Before moving forward, record the current objective, the last safe point, the visible route lock, and whether the next path is required or optional. Keep a short route log after every major interaction or movement unlock. That makes backtracking safer without turning an unverified assumption into a walkthrough fact.

<Callout kind="tip">Use the floor order as a research-based route scaffold. Verify every door, key, combat encounter, mechanism, and checkpoint in-game before relying on it for a blind run.</Callout>
