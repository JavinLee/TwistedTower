## Confirmed combat picture

Twisted Tower is a first-person action-adventure horror shooter set in an abandoned 1950s resort. The official material describes corrupted fairy-tale mascots, deadly traps, puzzles, and punchy, bloody gunplay across five major areas. The game also includes toy-themed weapons and movement toys, so preparation is about reading the arena and using the tools already confirmed for the current route.

The official store material lists weapon directions such as a melee mallet, rubber-band pistol, dart-throwing Tommy Gun, shotgun, chain-gun, sniper slingshot, and ray gun. Research notes warn not to publish exact damage values, named abilities, or transcript-derived mechanics without gameplay verification. These names can be used as equipment references, not as proof that one weapon is mandatory for every boss.

## Final fight research

The verified Tower Top walkthrough identifies Charlotte's Castle and the Tower Top ticket before the final encounter. The researched fight centers on caged torches, red weak points on a hot-air balloon, and rotating laser attacks. These details are guide-source material, not Steam documentation, so keep the source label visible when publishing them.

The collected preparation advice is practical but bounded. Finish upgrades before entering Tower Top and carry enough healing items according to the guide material. Watch for falls while moving around changing arena structures, and use jumps or double jumps to avoid rotating laser lines in the middle section. The source mentions the Cannon's secondary fire, Marble Gun, or Slingshot as recommendations, but no single loadout should be presented as mandatory.
