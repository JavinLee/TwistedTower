Welcome to the Into Indie Games walkthrough covering the Fairground Forest area of Twisted Tower.

Vincent goes through the entire Clown Casino but still can’t find any clue about Charlotte. To his surprise, using one of his Twisted Tokens allows him to reach her cage, only for the Twisted Beast to take her away once again.

Things take another turn when Mr. Twister himself appears in one of his recorded TV messages and invites Vincent outside to meet him. With no other choice, Vincent follows his instructions and makes his way toward the Fairground Forest, hoping this time he can finally get closer to finding Charlotte.

This is part of our Twisted Tower – Walkthrough Hub, so if you want more content about the game, be sure to check it out.

Exit The Tower And Enter The Fairground Forest

This time around, you don’t need a ticket to reach your next location, as it’s actually outside the tower. So, exit the tower. In case you missed the exit, the path will be marked by a trail of tickets.
At the entrance, where the ticket booth is located, you will find a trampoline. Use it to access the booths, then press the green button here to open the gate leading to the Fairground Forest.
The path here is straightforward, so simply walk forward until you come across the Cupid’s Catapult door. You will notice that it is missing two seats.

Find The Two Missing Seats

Walk up the hill and you will come across your first enemy with a bubble around it.
This is a shield, and you will need to destroy it before you can damage the enemy. This simply means you will need to conserve more ammunition.
At the top of the hill, the path splits in two directions:
Take the left path, following the wooden planks on the ground. There are enemies along the way, so be prepared to fight.
At the end of the path, you will find one of the two missing seats. Pick it up and take it back to the Cupid’s Catapult door.
Backtrack to the start and this time take the right path. Break the wooden planks and you will find the second seat just below you. Pick it up and place it on the door.
With all three seats intact, you can use them to jump over the door and progress forward.

Go Through The Cupid’s Catapult

You will find a tape sitting on the desk that you can listen to, giving you more information about your story with Charlotte.
Moving along, go through the circus tent and past the carousel until you spot another tent at the end of the path.
There are a lot of enemies here, specifically those annoying giraffes that can snipe you from the high ground. Be sure to take them out first.
As soon as you get closer to the tent, the gate will close and even more enemies will spawn. Defeat all of them and the gate will lower.
Inside, you will get a new weapon, the Slingshot. This is a high-damage weapon, so keep it for when you need it the most.
Before you leave the tent, press the green button in the middle to unlock the Thunder Tracks gate, which you probably noticed as you made your way here.

Go Through The Thunder Tracks Gate

As soon as you try to enter past the gate, there will be a turret that locks onto you and starts shooting.
If you’re not careful, this can melt through your health quickly. Use your Slingshot and aim to destroy it with two direct shots.
Progress forward and you will find another locked gate. Go to the right, but be careful, as there is another turret on this passage. Make sure to destroy it using the Slingshot.
At the end of this passage, you will find a trampoline. Use it to reach the upper floor. From here, jump across until you can reach the area behind the locked gate, then drop down.
You will find another cart here, similar to the one that was in the Casino. Get inside and use the middle stick to move forward. Sit tight until you reach a secret room.
Here, there will be a green button that you can press to unlock the Ferris Wheel gate. Use the cart to leave this location and then enter the next area.

Go Through The Ferris Wheel

At the entrance, you will find another TV. Mr. Twister is preparing another film, and it is currently in production.
It’s called Return to the Twisted Tower, which would be based on his very first movie that was successful.
Anyway, walk toward the wheel and you will find a Real Boy Station here.
It’s been a while since you’ve found one, and you probably already have a bunch of tickets to spend. Make sure to purchase any upgrades you need before moving on.

Go Through The Godmother Maze

When you’re ready, go behind the wheel and head toward the Grandmother Maze. Of course, when you get close, the gate will close and a bunch of enemies will appear.
Defeat them all and the gate will unlock itself. You will find Piper at the entrance, and it’s been a while since you’ve met the fella.
Piper, once again and somehow, knows exactly where Charlotte is. She has been carried by the Twisted Beast to Lightspeed Land.
Great, every time Vincent tries to get closer, Mr. Twister pushes the finish line even further away.
We have to go through the maze and obtain the key so we can head back to the tower.
As the name suggests, this area is a Garden Maze, so it is easy to get lost. We got lost here too, but the following directions should help you navigate through it. Follow the turns exactly in this order:

Right, second left, left, right, right, left, right, right, left, left.
Be careful, as the right path has a turret waiting for you.
Continue with left, right, left, second right. Watch out for the first right path, as there is another turret at the end.
Finally, take left, right, left, left. There is a turret waiting here, so go directly to the second left path.

If you follow these directions exactly, you should make it through the maze and find yourself out on the other side.
In the next section, there will be two walls and a turret. Trigger the walls so they go down, then quickly take cover. Destroy the turret and then interact with the lever here.

Head Back To The Tower

This will cause the Ferris Wheel to turn upside down, which will allow you to reach the top. Now, as you backtrack, you will be chased by spiders, so be careful not to get killed.
Once you’re outside of the maze, you will be able to climb up the Ferris Wheel using the grapple targets and trampolines. At the top, you will find the key, which you can use to unlock the gate and head back to the tower.
No need to jump back down. Instead, use the round platform on the ground to transport you right in front of the door. Use the key to unlock it, and you will find your final Twisted Token waiting for you.

Return To The Lobby And Use Your Twisted Token

Before you leave, there is another tape on the desk that you can listen to. When you’re done, exit the forest to go back inside the Twisted Tower.
Here, interact with the lever to open the gate, and you will be back at the entrance. Head toward the lobby and go to the upper floor where the elevator is.
To the right side, you will find a trampoline. Jump to the other side and unlock the door using your Twisted Token.
Head downstairs and you will find another tape that you can listen to.
Behind it, there is a path. Jump across and grapple to reach the other side, where you will find your final new toy, the Jetpack. This will allow you to double jump.
Using all your movement abilities, you will need to reach the top of this area to find the ticket to Lightspeed Land.
Use the double jump to reach the platform just above where the Jetpack was and press the green button. This will reveal the spot where the Explosive Dice will spawn.
Now, using this dice, you need to break the red wall on the top-right side of the room. Make your way there and turn the valve all the way to the end. This will reveal a platform, but as usual, it’s on a timer.
Look back and go to the platform ahead. From there, make a circle around to reach the center and then make your way to the upper area.
Here, you can spot the second valve behind the bar, so jump across and make your way to the end.
This will unlock the passage on the higher floor. Head back to where you were and go right, then jump across and be careful of the spiked wall.
You will reach the top, where you will finally find the ticket to Lightspeed Land.

Welcome to the Into Indie Games walkthrough covering the end of Twisted Tower.

Vincent has finally made it to the top of the Twisted Tower, and there is nowhere left to run. It’s time to confront Mr. Twister, defeat the Twisted Beast, and finally rescue his fiancée, Charlotte.

After chasing Charlotte through every twisted corner of the tower, Vincent is finally at the end of the road. Everything has led to this final confrontation, and now it’s time to put an end to Mr. Twister’s plans and save Charlotte once and for all.

Table of contents

Go Through Charlotte’s Castle

Just when you get outside of the Lightspeed Land, you will reach what we can tell from the sign is Charlotte’s Castle, and of course, yet another TV from Mr. Twister.
Mr. Twisted is impressed by how far Vincent has made it and says he will be waiting for you.
Follow the way to the right and defeat the enemies around. Look to the edge on the right side, and you will be able to see a caged torch on the other platform.
Jump across and activate it, then quickly backtrack to the gate and look to the top left. Grapple here to reach the upper floor.
More enemies will be around here, and you need to defeat them. When you’re done, jump to the upper battlement and platform your way to the battlement where you spot the caged torch.
Activate this caged torch, and then keep platforming through the battlement until you spot the grapple point on the other side. Grapple there.
Jump using the trampoline and make your way up until you reach the battlement here. You will find a Real Boy Station, so make sure to get any upgrades you need because this is the last one you will encounter.
Drop down and keep working your way through the battlements until you reach the elevator. Just outside, you will be able to pick up the ticket, and there is another TV here, the last one from Mr. Twister.
Strange enough, Mr. Twister is speaking to Vincent as if he is his son, and calls him Vincent Twister. This cannot be. Vincent never knew his father and only lived with his mother; this has to be another trick.
Go inside the elevator, insert the ticket, pick Tower Top, and be prepared for a fight.

Defeat Mr. Twister

You will reach the very top of the tower where Charlotte’s cage is there. Vincent runs toward her, and just as he tries to figure out how to get her out, Mr. Twister himself arrives on a hot air balloon.
He will hook Charlotte’s cage to the balloon and float around. It will shoot projectiles at you, but they are easy to avoid.
The whole boss fight requires you to find the caged torches around and activate them. This will reveal weak points on the balloon, marked by red dots, and you need to shoot and destroy every one of them.
If you upgraded the Cannon’s secondary fire, this will make your life easy since all the projectiles are tracking, so you won’t have to aim. Otherwise, the Marble Gun and the Slingshot are perfect for this.
Sounds simple enough. However, Mr. Twister will change the placement of the caged torch and mess around by changing the tower formation, so you need to be mindful of where you step and jump accordingly so you won’t fall.
Halfway through the fight, the balloon will station itself at the center and emit rotating crosshairs of laser beams. You can simply avoid these by jumping or double jumping if needed.
That’s pretty much the whole fight. After you destroy all the red dots on the balloon, it will fall, the cage holding Charlotte will be broken, and she will be free.

The Twist Of Mr. Twister’s Twisted Tower

Just as Vincent approaches Charlotte, he urges her to leave this tower once and for all.
But to his surprise, she holds his hand, and he notices that both of her hands are intact. If you remember, they cut off one of her hands during the Mermaid Palace.
This is when Charlotte reveals the whole thing. Numerous cameras are recording the scene as they start rolling, with Charlotte delivering her final line: “My heart, my lips, my body. They’re yours.”
Right after that, Mr. Twister will shout “Cut!” and deliver the final scene. It seems that Vincent was played by every single person he met, including Piper, his grandma, and even Charlotte.
They just wanted to help their father, Mr. Twister, make another banger of a movie, just like his first one, The Twisted Tower. And yes indeed, Vincent is his son.
And that’s all. Quite the twist, but it leaves quite a few plot holes and unanswered questions.
